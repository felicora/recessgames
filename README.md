# 🛝 Recess Games

### Childhood games we played between classes.

Remember when an eraser wasn't just an eraser?

**Recess Games** brings simple childhood desk games back to life as an interactive web experience.

Explore an illustrated school desk, hover over the objects and discover five nostalgic mini-games hidden inside.

## 🎮 What's on the desk?

🔮 **Paper Fortune Teller**  
Pick. Count. Unfold. Discover your fortune.

🥢 **Pick-Up Sticks**  
Remove one without moving the others.

✏️ **Eraser Battle**  
Flip your eraser onto your opponent's.

📓 **Square Grid**  
Connect. Capture. Win.

🥤 **Bottle Cap Game · Ceper**  
Toss. Scatter. Strike.

## ✨ How it works

The desk **is** the menu.

Objects lift and animate when you discover them. Click one to enter its mini-game, complete it, then return to the desk and find another.

Finish all five to unlock the final memory.

## 🎨 Made to feel handmade

Recess Games combines:

- hand-drawn game art
- school notebook textures
- ballpoint-pen doodles
- wooden desk scratches
- animated objects
- nostalgic classroom details
- warm afternoon light

No complicated progression.

No coins.

No battle pass.

Just five little games and one very familiar school desk.
